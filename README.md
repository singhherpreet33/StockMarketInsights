# StockMarketInsights

A stock market analysis and investment insights platform designed to help users research stocks, analyze market trends, and understand financial data.

## Features

* 📊 Stock market analysis
* 📈 Market trend tracking
* 🔍 Stock research and insights
* 💰 Investment-related data analysis
* 🇮🇳 Indian stock market focus
* 🤖 AI-powered analysis (if applicable)

## Purpose

The goal of this project is to provide useful tools and insights for understanding stock market data and making more informed investment research decisions.

## Disclaimer

This project is for **educational and research purposes only**. It does not provide financial advice or guarantee investment returns. Always conduct your own research before making investment decisions.
